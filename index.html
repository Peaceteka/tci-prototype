Working with communities in Nyakach for a healthier, more hopeful future

Tumaini Community Initiative (TCI) is a community-based organisation founded in 2018 by residents of Nyakach Sub-County. We work with families, schools and local leaders to bring safe water, keep girls in school and help young people earn a living.

Our programmes

  • Safe Water and Sanitation

    Community water points, household water treatment and school latrines.

    Read about the water programme

  • Girls' Education Support

    Bursaries, mentorship and sanitary supplies for girls in secondary school.

    Read about the education programme

  • Youth Livelihoods

    Skills training, savings groups and start-up kits for young people aged 18–35.

    Read about the livelihoods programme

Latest results

In 2025, 1,150 households had access to safe water through TCI water points, up from 310 in 2022. See our full 2025 programme results.
