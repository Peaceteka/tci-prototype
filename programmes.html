Our programmes

TCI runs three programmes. Each is planned with the community and reviewed every year using the results we publish.

Safe Water and Sanitation

We build and maintain community water points, train households to treat drinking water, and construct latrines in primary schools. Each water point is run by an elected water committee that collects a small fee for maintenance.

Girls' Education Support

We provide bursaries and mentorship so that girls from low-income households can complete secondary school.

How to apply for a bursary

  1. Collect an application form from your school or the TCI office in Pap Onditi.
  2. Ask the head teacher to confirm your enrolment and sign the form.
  3. Attach a letter from your area chief confirming household need.
  4. Return the form before 31 January.
  5. Shortlisted applicants are invited to an interview in February.

Youth Livelihoods

Young people aged 18–35 train in skills such as tailoring, solar installation and poultry keeping, join savings groups, and receive a start-up kit when they complete the course.

Key terms we use

Water point
A community-managed kiosk with a storage tank and taps that supplies treated water.
Bursary
Money paid directly to a school to cover part or all of a student's fees.
Savings group
A group of 15–25 members who save regularly and lend to each other at agreed rates.
Beneficiary
A person or household that directly receives a TCI service.
